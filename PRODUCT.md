# Reparto: product one-pager

**What it is:** Drag people onto deliverables and see the capacity gaps

**Who it is for:** An engineering manager or tech lead the week before quarter
planning, with a list of deliverables, a team with holidays and vacations in it,
and no honest number yet for how much of that list fits.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A laptop on a desk in the planning week, often screen-shared into a
call with the team or a manager, iterated for twenty minutes: move a person, size a
deliverable, watch a flag go away. Dense and dark like the rest of the fleet, and it
must read clearly when shared at a smaller size.

**What done looks like:** Every deliverable is sized and staffed, nobody is booked past
their capacity, and the flags panel says "all clear", or the manager leaves with the
exact gap ("29 points short, about one engineer") to take into the planning meeting.
